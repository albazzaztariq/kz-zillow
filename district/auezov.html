Положение найдено по данным OpenStreetMap: точное, по номеру дома без корпуса или по похожему названию улицы. Each dot is one building, coloured by the median ₸/m² of its market sales; zoomed out the dots merge into larger groups and split into single buildings as you zoom in. Addresses exist only in the files up to 2024Q3 (newer files do not publish an address), so recent sales are not shown on the map. Locations come from OpenStreetMap: exact, by the base house number, or by a similar street spelling.
